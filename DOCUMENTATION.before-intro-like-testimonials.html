Video Gallery Block: Getting Started

You have videos worth showing, but a list of links can be hard for visitors to browse. Video Gallery Block brings those videos together on your WordPress page, where people can scan thumbnails, filter by topic and open a video without losing their place.

You can build your first gallery without writing code or creating an API key. Start with one video, make sure it appears on your page, then add the organization and design options your site needs.

The free version covers the everyday job: bring videos together, help visitors find the right one, and make the gallery fit your site. Optional privacy, analytics and search settings are there when you need them.

What You Can Do with the Free Version

  • Add YouTube, Vimeo and Facebook videos to one gallery.
  • Organize videos into albums and let visitors filter them.
  • Import many video links at once with Bulk Import.
  • Play videos in a lightbox, which is a popup player that opens over the page.
  • Adjust columns, spacing, colors and captions for desktop, tablet and mobile.
  • Use privacy, Google Analytics and Video SEO options when you need them.

Start Here

If the plugin is not installed yet, start with Installation and Activation. If it is already active, go straight to Create Your First Video Gallery. If you want more layouts, see Set Up Video Gallery Pro.

Installation and Activation

Before you build a gallery, make sure the plugin is available in your WordPress editor. This is a one-time setup; after activation, you can move straight to creating your first gallery.

Install the Plugin

Use WordPress's built-in plugin installer; it downloads and prepares the plugin for you.

  1. Open Plugins in the left menu, then click Add Plugin (the menu item or the button at the top).
  2. Type Video Gallery Block in the Search plugins box.
  3. Find the plugin by bPlugins and click Install Now.
  4. Click Activate when the button changes.

Make Sure the Plugin Is Active

After activation, look for Video Gallery in the left side of your WordPress dashboard. Seeing that menu is the sign that setup is complete.

Tip

If you received a ZIP file instead, open Plugins > Add New Plugin > Upload Plugin, choose the file and install it.

Now turn the empty page into a working gallery: Create Your First Video Gallery.

Create Your First Video Gallery

The quickest way to learn the block is to publish a simple gallery first. Once you have seen it on your page, you can add more videos and explore the settings without guessing what each option changes.

Add the Video Gallery Block to a Page

  1. Go to Pages in the left menu, then click Add Page (the menu item or the button at the top).
  2. The editor opens with an empty page. Click the + button at the top left.
  3. Type Video Gallery Block in the search box.
  4. Click Video Gallery Block to add it to the page.
  5. In the right sidebar, open the General tab, then the Videos panel.
  6. Paste a YouTube or Vimeo link into the Video url box.
  7. Click Publish, or Update if the page is already live.

For YouTube and Vimeo, the plugin usually fills in the title and thumbnail for you, so you can see the gallery take shape with very little setup.

Your first gallery is live. That is the first milestone. Next, add the videos you actually want visitors to watch; you can organize and style the gallery once the content is in place.

Start with a Ready-Made Pattern (Optional)

Prefer a ready-made design? A pattern is a layout you can insert instead of starting from a blank page. If you already published the simple gallery above, treat these steps as an alternative way to start another gallery.

  1. Click the + button at the top, then open the Patterns tab.
  2. Pick the Video Galleries category.
  3. Pick a pattern. Try Video gallery with album filters.
  4. Click Edit pattern, then click the gallery. Replace the sample videos in the Videos panel with your own.

Available Gallery Patterns

  • Video gallery with album filters: three columns and three albums.
  • Course video library: four columns, filtered by module.
  • Product demo showcase: three large tiles.
  • Customer video stories: video testimonials with the speaker named.
  • Portfolio showreel wall: four tight columns, no captions.
Tip

The sample videos are only placeholders. Replace every one you want to keep in the layout before publishing, or visitors may see the samples.

Now fill the gallery with your own content: Add Videos to Your Gallery.

Add Videos to Your Gallery

Your first gallery gives you the starting point. Now add the videos people came to see: add them one at a time when you want control, or use Bulk Import when you already have a list of links.

Add One Video

  1. Select the gallery, then open the General tab in the right sidebar.
  2. To add a video, click the plus (+) button in the block toolbar.

    Or click Add New Video at the bottom of the Videos panel.

  3. Paste the link into the Video url box inside the Videos panel.

You can use YouTube, Vimeo or Facebook links, including Facebook Reels. The thumbnail is the preview image people see before playing; the caption is the text shown with it.

Change the Thumbnail and Caption

YouTube and Vimeo usually supply a title and thumbnail automatically. Keep them for speed, or replace them when you want a more consistent look across your gallery.

  1. Click the Poster url box, then paste an image link or choose an image from your Media Library.
  2. Type your own text in Caption:.
Tip

Facebook links do not fill in the title or poster automatically, so add those details yourself.

Where the Thumbnail Is Saved

Downloaded thumbnails are saved in your WordPress Media Library, the same place you manage the other images on your site.

Add Many Videos with Bulk Import

Already have a list of links? Bulk Import saves you from repeating the same add-video steps for every item.

Import Multiple Video Links

  1. Select the gallery and open the General tab.
  2. Open the Import Videos panel.
  3. Paste your links into Video links, one link on each line.
  4. Click Import videos.

After the import, a message reports how many videos were added and how many received a title and poster.

Good to know

  • Links already in the gallery are skipped.
  • Lines that are not links are skipped.
  • If every link is a YouTube Short, the thumbnail shape switches to 9:16.

Once the gallery has several videos, help visitors find the right one by grouping related clips into albums and adding filters.

Organize Videos with Albums, Filters & Lightbox

A few videos are easy to browse. As the gallery grows, visitors need a quick way to find a topic instead of scanning every thumbnail. Start by grouping related clips into albums, then let visitors filter by topic.

An album is a group of related videos, such as Tutorials, Products or Customer Stories. The filter bar displays the album names as buttons visitors can choose.

Create an Album

  1. Open the General tab, then the Add or Remove Albums panel.
  2. Click Add New Album.
  3. Type a name in the new box.

To remove an album you no longer need, click the small cross next to its name.

Add a Video to an Album

  1. Open a video in the Videos panel.
  2. Tick the boxes for the albums you want under Select Albums:.

A video can belong to more than one album, so the same clip can appear under more than one topic.

Let Visitors Filter and Watch Videos

Once the albums are assigned, visitors can choose a topic from the filter bar. When they click a thumbnail, the lightbox opens the video in a popup so they can watch without leaving the page.

Show or Hide the Filter Bar

  1. Open the General tab, then the Filter panel.
  2. Switch Show Filter Bar on or off.
  3. Change the text in Common Filter. It is the button that shows every video. The default is All Videos.

The filter bar appears only when the gallery has at least one album. If you cannot see it, check that an album exists and that a video has been assigned to it.

Change the Filter Button Style

  1. Open the Style tab, then the Filter panel.
  2. Set Button Colors for the normal look and Button Hover/Active Colors for the chosen button.
  3. Change the font and size with Button Typography:.

How the Lightbox Works

No separate setup is needed for the lightbox. Visitors can move between videos with the arrows or close the popup with the cross.

Style the Lightbox Caption

  1. Open the Style tab, then the LightBox Caption panel.
  2. Change Caption Typography:.
  3. Change Caption Colors:.
  4. Change Caption Padding:.

Show or Style the Play Icon

The play icon is a visual cue that a tile contains a video. Keep it visible if visitors might mistake the thumbnail for a regular image, or adjust its size and color to match your design.

  1. Open the General tab, then the Options panel.
  2. Switch Show Play Icon on Thumbnail on or off.
  3. Open the Style tab, then the Item panel.
  4. Change Play Icon Colors:, then Play Icon Size:.
  5. Change Play Icon Hover Zoom:.

Your videos are now easier to browse. If you want to reuse this same gallery on another page, continue to Show a Gallery Anywhere with Shortcodes.

Show a Gallery Anywhere with Shortcodes

Once one gallery looks right, you do not need to rebuild it for every page. A shortcode is a small piece of text in square brackets that tells WordPress to display a saved gallery wherever you place it.

For example, [video_gallery id=12] tells WordPress to show the gallery saved with that ID. Your number may be different.

Save a Gallery and Copy Its Shortcode

  1. Open Video Gallery in the left menu, then click Add New Video Gallery.
  2. Add your videos in the block, then click Publish.
  3. Click the copy button next to the shortcode bar at the top of the editor.

Paste the Shortcode on Another Page

  1. Edit any page, click the + button, and type Shortcode in the search box.
  2. Click the Shortcode block to add it to the page.
  3. The block appears with an empty box that says Write shortcode here. Click inside that box.
  4. Paste the line, for example [video_gallery id=12]. Your number will differ.
  5. Click Publish.
  6. Click View Page. It is in the notice at the bottom of the screen, and also as an icon in the top bar.
  7. The page opens in a new tab. The gallery now shows where you pasted the line.
Tip

In the editor, the shortcode looks like plain text. On the live page, visitors see the actual gallery. You can also copy it from the Shortcode column on the Video Gallery list page.

That is the benefit: one saved gallery can appear on several pages. Update it once, and every page using its shortcode displays the updated version. Next, make its layout and appearance fit your site in Customize Gallery Layout & Style.

Customize Gallery Layout & Style

Your videos are in place and visitors can find them. Now make the gallery comfortable to browse on desktop, tablet and phone: set the layout first, then the thumbnails, then the visual details that make it feel part of your site.

Adjust Columns for Desktop, Tablet and Mobile

A desktop can fit more columns; a phone usually needs fewer so each thumbnail stays easy to see. Set the column count separately for each device.

  1. Open the General tab, then the Layout Settings panel.
  2. Pick Desktop, Tablet or Mobile with the device icon next to Columns:.
  3. Drag the slider to choose 1 to 6 columns.

Adjust the Space Between Videos

  1. Pick a device, then type a number in Column Gap:. This is the space left and right.
  2. Type a number in Row Gap:. This is the space above and below.

Align the Last Row

If the final row has fewer videos than the rows above it, choose whether those remaining tiles sit on the left, in the center or on the right.

  1. Choose Left, Center or Right under Row Alignment:.

Match the Popup to the Video Shape

Portrait and landscape videos have different proportions. Turn this on when you want the popup player to follow each video's shape instead of forcing every video into the same frame.

  1. Open the General tab, then the Layout Settings panel.
  2. Switch on Popup video width as aspect ratio.

Choose the Video Order

  1. Choose Manual, Newest First or Oldest First under Sort Order:.

Choose Manual when the sequence matters and you want to keep the order you set. Use Newest First or Oldest First when date order is more useful.

Show Fewer Videos First with Load More

For a long gallery, show a smaller first group instead of making visitors scroll past every video at once. The Load More button lets them decide when to see the next group.

  1. Type a number in Videos Per Page:. Zero shows every video.
  2. Change Load More Button Text: for your own wording. It appears once a number is set.

Choose the Thumbnail Shape

Choose a thumbnail shape that suits the videos. A consistent shape makes the grid look tidy; 9:16 is suited to tall Shorts and Reels.

  1. Open the Style tab, then the Item panel.
  2. Choose Fixed, 16:9, 4:3, 1:1 or 9:16 under Thumbnail Shape:.
  3. With Fixed, type a number in the Height: box that appears.

Show All Videos or Only YouTube Shorts

  1. Open the General tab, then the Options panel.
  2. Choose All Videos, Hide Shorts or Only Shorts under YouTube Shorts:.

Control Thumbnail Fit and Captions

  1. In the Options panel, choose Contain, Cover, Fill or None under Poster Fit:. Cover fills the tile fully.
  2. Switch Show Caption on Thumbnail on to place each caption over its tile.

Change Colors, Borders, Fonts and Shadows

Once the layout feels right, match the gallery to your website. The Style tab lets you change the outer gallery area, each video tile and the captions independently.

Style the Gallery Area

The Gallery panel styles the area around the tiles, rather than the individual videos.

  1. Open the Style tab, then the Gallery panel.
  2. Pick a color or image under Background:.
  3. Set the space inside the box with Padding:.
  4. Add an outline and round the corners with Border:.
  5. Add a soft drop shadow with Shadow:.

Style Each Video Tile

These settings apply to each video tile, so use them to keep the thumbnails visually consistent.

  1. Open the Item panel in the Style tab.
  2. Round the corners or add an outline with Item Border:.
  3. Add a shadow under each tile with Item Shadow:.

Style the Captions

  1. In the Item panel, set the font with Caption Typography:.
  2. Set text and background colors with Caption Colors:.
  3. Set the space around the text with Caption Padding:.
Tip

Change one setting at a time and check the preview as you go. It is easier to spot which change improved the result.

The gallery is ready to publish. If your site needs visitor consent, playback measurement or extra video information for search engines, those optional settings are next: Privacy, Analytics & Video SEO.

Privacy, Analytics & Video SEO

Your gallery is ready. These optional settings control what happens around playback: whether visitors choose before a video loads, whether plays are sent to Google Analytics, and what information search engines receive about each video.

These are separate options, not a required setup sequence. Use only the ones your site needs; if a feature is not relevant, leave it off or keep its current setting.

Ask for Permission Before Loading a Video

YouTube, Vimeo and Facebook may set cookies when their players load. Enable this option if you want visitors to choose before the video player is loaded.

  1. Open the General tab, then the Options panel.
  2. Switch Require Consent Before Playing on.

Visitors see a notice naming the video host. They can choose Play Video to continue or Cancel to stop, and the browser remembers their choice.

Track Video Plays with Google Analytics

Google Analytics 4 (GA4) measures activity on your website. If GA4 is already connected, enable this option to send video-play events to it; this setting does not connect GA4 to your site by itself.

  1. In the Options panel, switch Send Video Play Events to Google Analytics on.

When enabled, the gallery sends the following events with the video title, host and link:

  • video_start when a visitor opens a video.
  • video_play when they press play.
  • video_watch_progress at 25, 50 and 75 percent.
  • video_watch_complete when the video ends.

To test it, open Google Analytics and go to Reports > Realtime. Then open your site in a private browser window and play a video.

Tip

Facebook videos send only video_start because Facebook does not provide the same play details. If you use Google Tag Manager, create a Custom Event trigger for each event name.

Help Search Engines Understand Your Videos

Video SEO markup is structured information added behind the page. It tells search engines details such as the video title, thumbnail and video link, helping them understand what the page contains.

Turn Video SEO Markup On or Off

This option is on by default. Turn it off only if another SEO plugin already adds video markup, so you do not create duplicate markup.

  1. Open the General tab, then the Options panel.
  2. Switch Video SEO markup on or off.

Give Search Engines Better Video Information

  1. Give every video a clear caption.
  2. Give every video a thumbnail.
  3. Use real video links, not links to other pages.

Plugin Dashboard & Settings

You will do most gallery work in the page editor. Open the Video Gallery dashboard when you need plugin-wide information, demos, block controls or a setting that affects saved data.

Open the Video Gallery Dashboard

  1. Click Video Gallery in the left menu.
  2. Click Help & Demos.

The page has Welcome, Blocks, Demos, Pricing, Feature Comparison and Settings tabs. On a small screen, use the menu button to reveal them.

Choose What Happens to Saved Data If You Delete the Plugin

Deleting a plugin is different from deactivating it. By default, your saved galleries are kept when the plugin is uninstalled. Turn on Delete plugin data on uninstall only if you want those saved galleries removed too.

  1. Open the Settings tab.
  2. Switch the toggle under Delete plugin data on uninstall. Confirm the warning.
Warning

If this option is on, uninstalling permanently removes the plugin's saved gallery data. Deactivating the plugin does not delete it, so check this setting carefully before uninstalling.

Set Up Video Gallery Pro

Consider Pro when the standard gallery no longer fits the experience you want to build. Pro adds more layout choices and features for stores and visitor interaction.

The steps will feel familiar: add a block, replace its sample videos, adjust the settings, preview and publish.

What Pro Adds

See the Pro Summary in the Free Plugin

The free plugin cannot show the Pro blocks themselves. Check Pro Version opens a short summary card, so you can see what Pro adds before you decide.

  2. Click Check Pro Version in the block toolbar.

Install Video Gallery Pro

Use the Pro ZIP file from your purchase. You do not need to delete your existing free galleries or remove the free plugin by hand.

  1. Open Plugins and click Add Plugin (the menu item or the button at the top).
  2. Click Upload Plugin.
  3. Click Choose File, pick the Pro zip file from your purchase, then click Install Now.
  4. Click Activate Plugin.
  5. Go to Plugins and check that Video Gallery Block Pro is active. The free copy switches itself off. That is normal.

Activate Your Pro License

Your license key connects this website to your Pro purchase so it can receive Pro updates and support.

  1. Open Video Gallery and click Help & Demos.
  2. Click the Activation tab.
  3. Paste your key into the License key box.
  4. Click Activate License. The page then shows Activated.

Moving to another site? Click Deactivate license here first.

Hide Pro Blocks You Do Not Use

If the block inserter becomes crowded, hide the Pro blocks you do not plan to use. This changes what appears in the editor; it does not remove the blocks from the plugin.

  1. Open Video Gallery and click Help & Demos.

  3. Turn the switch off on any block you want to hide.

Choose a Layout from the Layout Picker

  1. Open Video Gallery and click Add New Video Gallery.

Create Your First Pro Gallery

This walkthrough uses the Video Slider as an example. You will add the block, replace the sample videos, adjust its design and publish. Then you can use the same rhythm with other Pro blocks.

Add a Pro Block

Every Pro block lives in the inserter, under the Video Gallery category.

  1. Open a page in the editor and click the + button at the top left.
  2. Type Video Slider in the search box and click the block.

The block includes sample videos so you can preview the layout before adding your own. Replace each sample you want to keep; any sample left in the block will appear on the published page.

Add Your Videos

Replace the samples with your own videos. For source options and thumbnail guidance, see Add Videos to Your Gallery.

  1. Click the block to select it, then open the General tab.
  2. Open the Videos panel and click the first video.
  3. Paste your link into the Video box. It can be a YouTube link, a Vimeo link or a file from your Media Library.
  4. Type a Title. Add a Poster image if you want your own thumbnail.
  5. Click Add New Video to add another one.
Tip

Delete the samples you do not replace. Anything left behind gets published.

Adjust the Layout and Style

  1. Open Options on the General tab for autoplay and video fit.
  2. Open Layout Settings for gaps and sizes.
  3. Open the Style tab for colours, fonts and borders.

Preview and Publish

  1. Click Preview and check the page at phone size too.
  2. Click Publish or Update.

Product Videos & Visitor Engagement

A video can do more than play. It can help someone understand a product, or invite them to react to what they watch. This section covers both uses: product videos first, then views, likes, saves and comments.

Before You Use WooCommerce Product Videos

WooCommerce is the WordPress plugin used to run an online store. It must be installed and active to connect a video to an existing store product. If you do not use WooCommerce, you can still enter a title, price and product link yourself.

Connect a Video to a WooCommerce Product

  1. Insert the Video Carousel Gallery block and open a video in the Videos panel.
  2. Switch on Use WooCommerce Product.
  3. Search for your product and choose it in Select Product.

Once linked, the card pulls the product title, price, sale price and image from WooCommerce. That means you do not have to update those details separately in the gallery.

Add Product Details Manually

  1. Leave Use WooCommerce Product off.
  2. Add the Product Image and the Title.
  3. Type the Current Price and the Original Price.
  4. Paste the Product URL.
  5. With WooCommerce active, click Create Product to make a store product from those details.

Adjust Product Carousel Options

  1. Type the sign before prices in Currency Symbol.
  2. Switch on Open in New Tab to keep the gallery open when the shop link is clicked.
  3. Switch on Autoplay Previews to play each card muted as it scrolls into view.

Product videos help visitors decide what to buy. If your goal is interaction instead, the Video Shorts Reel can let visitors react to a clip, save it for later or leave a comment.

Show Views, Likes, Saves & Comments

View counts show how often a video has been played. The Video Shorts Reel adds ways for visitors to respond: like a clip, save it for later or comment on it.

Show View Counts in a Playlist

Counts use short labels such as 12K views. They start at zero on your site and increase as videos are played there.

  1. Click the Video Playlist Gallery block to select it.
  2. Open Options on the General tab.
  3. Switch on View/Upload Date.

Let Visitors Like, Save and Comment on Shorts

Anyone can like a video. Visitors must be logged in to save a video or post a comment.

  1. Select the Video Shorts Reel block.
  2. Switch on Show Action Rail.

You do not need to switch on the save bookmark separately. Logged-in visitors see it on the card automatically; use the Saved Button panel in the Style tab to change its appearance.

Show a Logged-In Visitor Their Saved Videos

Add [vsr_saved_videos] to a page with a Shortcode block to show the logged-in visitor a grid of videos they have saved. This is separate from the gallery shortcode: it displays that visitor's saved list, not a specific gallery.

Manage Likes and Comments

  1. Open Video Gallery and click Reel Moderation.
  2. Delete one comment, delete all comments, or reset the likes.

Common Problems and Fixes

Match the symptom to the heading below and try that fix first. Reload the editor or live page afterward to check whether it solved the problem.

Pro Blocks Still Look Locked After Upgrading

The license may not be active. Open Video Gallery > Help & Demos > Activation and check that the status says Activated. See Activate Your Pro License.

My Pro License Will Not Activate

Read the message on the Activation tab. It should identify the issue, for example, a mistyped key, an expired license or no activations remaining. Correct that issue and try again.

A Video Block Is Missing from the Inserter

The block may be hidden in the plugin settings. Open Video Gallery > Help & Demos > Blocks, turn that block on, then reload the editor. See Hide Pro Blocks You Do Not Use for the same setting.

A Video Shows a Blank Thumbnail

Select the video, open its settings and add an image in the Poster field. That image is used as the thumbnail before playback.

A Video Will Not Autoplay with Sound

Most browsers block videos from autoplaying with sound before a visitor interacts with the page. Keep autoplay muted, or let visitors press Play themselves.
