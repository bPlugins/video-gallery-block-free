Video Gallery Block: Introduction

Turn your YouTube and Vimeo links into one clean gallery. Visitors can filter it and play every video in a popup, without leaving the page.

You build it in the block editor. No code, no API key.

What You Get for Free

  • YouTube, Vimeo and Facebook videos in one gallery.
  • Albums and filter buttons, so visitors can find a topic fast.
  • Bulk Import. Paste a list of links and the gallery fills itself.
  • A lightbox that plays each video in a popup.
  • Columns for each device, plus spacing, borders and shadows.
  • Privacy and tracking options: ask for consent before a player loads, and send plays to Google Analytics.

What Pro Adds

Video Gallery Block Pro adds 31 more blocks, such as sliders, masonry grids, playlists and video stories. It also adds self-hosted and Wistia videos.

You can use Pro blocks next to the free one on the same page. See Video Gallery Block Pro.

Where to Go Next

Start with the Quick Start. You will publish your first gallery in about five minutes. Not installed yet? See Installation and Activation.

Installation and Activation

Get Video Gallery Block on your site in about a minute. You do not need any code or any account.

Install from your dashboard

This is the easiest way. WordPress downloads the plugin for you.

  1. Open Plugins in the left menu, then click Add New Plugin.
  2. Type Video Gallery Block in the search box. Find the plugin by bPlugins and click Install Now.
  3. Click Activate when the button changes.

Check that it worked

A new Video Gallery item appears in your left menu. That means the plugin is ready.

Tip

Have a ZIP file instead? Go to Plugins, Add New Plugin, then Upload Plugin. Choose the ZIP, install it and click Activate.

Quick Start: Your First Gallery

A gallery lets visitors browse many videos in one tidy grid and play each one in a popup. Here is how to make your first one.

Add the block to a page

  1. Open any page or post in the block editor.
  2. Click the + button at the top, then search for Video Gallery Block and click it.
  3. In the right sidebar, open the General tab, then the Videos panel. Paste a YouTube or Vimeo link into the Video url box.
  4. Click Publish, or Update if the page is already live.

The title and thumbnail fill in by themselves for YouTube and Vimeo links.

Or start from a ready made pattern

Do not want a blank block? Patterns give you a finished section. You only swap in your own videos.

  1. Click the + button at the top, then open the Patterns tab.
  2. Pick the Video Galleries category.
  3. Click the pattern you like. Try Video gallery with album filters.
  4. Click the pattern, then click Edit pattern. Click the gallery, then replace the sample videos in the Videos panel with your own.

The five patterns

  • Video gallery with album filters: three columns and a filter bar with three albums.
  • Course video library: four columns, filtered by module.
  • Product demo showcase: three large tiles, no filter bar.
  • Customer video stories: video testimonials with the speaker named in each caption.
  • Portfolio showreel wall: a tight four column wall with no captions.
Tip

The sample videos in a pattern are placeholders. Replace each one before you publish.

Peek at Pro without buying

You can preview Pro from inside the editor. It costs nothing.

  1. Select the Video Gallery Block in the editor.
  2. Click Check Pro Version in the block toolbar.

Add Videos

Every video in your gallery has a link, a thumbnail and a caption. You can add them one at a time here, or paste many at once with Bulk Import.

Add one video

  1. Select the gallery, then open the General tab in the right sidebar.
  2. Click the plus button in the block toolbar. Its tooltip says Add New Video.
  3. Paste the link into the Video url box inside the Videos panel.

Works with YouTube, Vimeo and Facebook links.

Set the thumbnail and caption

For YouTube and Vimeo, the poster and the caption fill in for you. You can change them any time.

  1. Click the Poster url box, then paste an image link or choose an image from your Media Library.
  2. Type your own text in Caption:.
Tip

Facebook videos do not fill in a title or poster. Add a poster and a caption by hand for those.

Thumbnail pictures stay on your site

YouTube and Vimeo thumbnails are saved to your Media Library once. After that, your own site serves them, so pages stay quick.

Bulk Import

Adding twenty videos one by one is slow. Paste all the links at once and the gallery fills itself in.

Import many videos

  1. Select the gallery and open the General tab.
  2. Open the Import Videos panel.
  3. Paste your links into Video links, one link on each line.
  4. Click Import videos.

A message tells you how many videos were added and how many got a title and a poster.

Good to know

  • Links already in the gallery are skipped.
  • Lines that are not links are skipped.
  • If every link is a YouTube Short, the thumbnail shape switches to 9:16 for you.

Albums

Albums are groups, like Tutorials or Customer stories. They power the filter bar, so visitors can show just the group they care about.

Create an album

  1. Open the General tab, then the Add or Remove Albums panel.
  2. Click Add New Album.
  3. Type a name in the new box.

To delete an album, click the small cross next to its name.

Put a video in an album

  1. Open a video in the Videos panel.
  2. Tick the album under Select Albums:. A video can sit in more than one album.

Next, see Filter Bar and Lightbox to control how the buttons look and behave.

Filter Bar and Lightbox

The filter bar helps visitors find a video fast. The lightbox plays the video in a popup, so they never leave the page.

Show or hide the filter bar

  1. Open the General tab, then the Filter panel.
  2. Switch Show Filter Bar on or off.
  3. Change the text in Common Filter. This is the button that shows every video. The default is All Videos.

The bar only shows when the gallery has at least one album.

Change the button colors

  1. Open the Style tab, then the Filter panel.
  2. Set Button Colors for the normal look and Button Hover/Active Colors for the chosen button.

Use Button Typography: in the same panel to change the font and size.

The lightbox

When a visitor clicks a thumbnail, the video opens in a popup. Nothing to switch on.

  • A strip of small thumbnails shows where they are.
  • Arrow buttons move to the next or earlier video, and a cross closes the popup.

Style the caption inside the lightbox

  1. Open the Style tab, then the LightBox Caption panel.
  2. Change Caption Typography:, Caption Colors: and Caption Padding:.

The play icon

A play icon sits on every thumbnail, so it looks like a video right away.

  1. Open the General tab, then the Options panel.
  2. Switch Show Play Icon on Thumbnail on or off.
  3. Open the Style tab, then the Item panel. Change Play Icon Colors:, Play Icon Size: and Play Icon Hover Zoom:.

Reuse a Gallery with a Shortcode

Build a gallery once, then show it on many pages. A shortcode is a short line of text that places your saved gallery anywhere.

Create a saved gallery

  1. Open Video Gallery in the left menu, then click Add New Video Gallery.
  2. Add your videos in the block, then click Publish.
  3. Click the copy button next to the shortcode bar at the top of the editor.

Paste it on a page

  1. Edit any page and add a Shortcode block.
  2. Paste the line, for example [video_gallery id=12]. Your number will be different.
Tip

You can also copy the shortcode from the Shortcode column on the Video Gallery list page.

Layout Settings

Layout decides how many tiles sit in a row, how far apart they are and how big they look. Most of it lives in one panel.

Columns for each device

Phones need fewer columns than desktops. You can set each device on its own.

  1. Open the General tab, then the Layout Settings panel.
  2. Pick Desktop, Tablet or Mobile with the device icon next to Columns:.
  3. Drag the slider to choose 1 to 6 columns.

Space between tiles

  1. Pick a device, then type a number in Column Gap:. This is the space left and right.
  2. Type a number in Row Gap:. This is the space above and below.

Place the last row

If your last row is not full, you choose where its tiles sit.

  1. Choose Left, Center or Right under Row Alignment:.

Sort order

  1. Choose Manual, Newest First or Oldest First under Sort Order:.

Manual keeps the order you added the videos in.

Show a few, then Load More

A big gallery can feel heavy. Show a few videos first and let visitors ask for more.

  1. Type a number in Videos Per Page:. Zero shows every video.
  2. Change Load More Button Text: if you want your own wording. It appears once a number is set.

Thumbnail shape

The shape keeps every tile the same on all screens. Pick 9:16 for tall Shorts and Reels.

  1. Open the Style tab, then the Item panel.
  2. Choose Fixed, 16:9, 4:3, 1:1 or 9:16 under Thumbnail Shape:.

With Fixed, a Height: box appears so you can set a height yourself.

Show or hide YouTube Shorts

  1. Open the General tab, then the Options panel.
  2. Choose All Videos, Hide Shorts or Only Shorts under YouTube Shorts:.

Poster fit and captions on thumbnails

  1. In the Options panel, choose Contain, Cover, Fill or None under Poster Fit:. Cover fills the tile fully.
  2. Switch Show Caption on Thumbnail on to place each caption over its tile.

Styling

Make the gallery match your brand. Styling controls sit in the Style tab, split into the whole gallery and each tile.

Style the whole gallery

The gallery is the box around all your tiles.

  1. Open the Style tab, then the Gallery panel.
  2. Pick a color or image under Background:.
  3. Set the space inside the box with Padding:.
  4. Add an outline and round the corners with Border:.
  5. Add a soft drop shadow with Shadow:.

Style each tile

These apply to every thumbnail, separate from the box around them.

  1. Open the Item panel in the Style tab.
  2. Round the corners or add an outline with Item Border:.
  3. Add a shadow under each tile with Item Shadow:.

Style the captions

  1. In the Item panel, set the font with Caption Typography:.
  2. Set text and background colors with Caption Colors:.
  3. Set the space around the text with Caption Padding:.
Tip

Change one thing at a time and watch the editor. It updates as you go.

Privacy and Analytics

Two optional switches help with privacy rules and with measuring views. Both are off until you turn them on.

Ask before a video loads

YouTube, Vimeo and Facebook can set cookies when their player loads. This switch asks the visitor first, so nothing loads until they agree.

  1. Open the General tab, then the Options panel.
  2. Switch Require Consent Before Playing on.

Visitors see a notice that names the video host. They click Play Video to continue or Cancel to stop.

Once they agree, the browser remembers it, so they are not asked again.

Count video plays in Google Analytics

See which videos people actually watch. This works if Google Analytics 4 or Google Tag Manager is already on your site.

  1. In the Options panel, switch Send Video Play Events to Google Analytics on.

Each play sends a video_start event with the title, the host and the link. You do not need to set up anything else.

Tip

In Google Analytics, look for the video_start event to see your plays.

Video SEO

Google likes to know what a video is about. The plugin tells search engines the title, the thumbnail and the link for each video, so your gallery can show up as a video result.

Turn it on or off

It is on by default. You do nothing. Turn it off only if your SEO plugin already adds this for you.

  1. Open the General tab, then the Options panel.
  2. Switch Video SEO markup on or off.

Help it work better

  • Give every video a clear caption.
  • Give every video a thumbnail.
  • Use real video links, not links to other pages.

Dashboard and Help Page

The plugin adds one help page in your admin. It has demos, a Free and Pro comparison and one safety setting.

Open the page

  1. Click Video Gallery in the left menu.
  2. Click Help & Demos.

At the top you will find these tabs. On a narrow screen they sit behind the menu button.

  • Welcome and Blocks.
  • Demos to see live examples.
  • Pricing and Feature Comparison.
  • Settings.

Keep or delete your data on uninstall

By default your galleries stay if you remove the plugin. You can change that.

  1. Open the Settings tab.
  2. Switch the toggle under Delete plugin data on uninstall. Confirm the warning that appears.
Warning

With this on, deleting the plugin removes all your saved galleries for good. Turning the plugin off does not delete anything.

What Video Gallery Block Pro Adds

Pro adds 31 ready made video layouts to the free plugin. Pick the one that fits your page, add your videos, and publish.

What You Get

The free plugin gives you one gallery block. Pro gives you a whole shelf of blocks, from sliders and walls to stories, maps and menus.

  • Free: the Video Gallery block with albums, filters, a lightbox, bulk import and video SEO markup.
  • Pro: 31 more blocks, each built for one job.
  • Pro: WooCommerce product videos in the Video Carousel Gallery.
  • Pro: likes, saves and comments on the Video Shorts Reel.
  • Pro: Wistia videos in select blocks, plus priority support.

For the free block, see Add Your Videos and Set the Layout.

Install Pro Next to Free

You do not need to remove anything first. Pro carries its own copy of the Video Gallery block, so your saved galleries keep working.

  1. Open Plugins in the WordPress menu and click Add New Plugin, then Upload Plugin.
  2. Choose the Pro zip file from your purchase, click Install Now, then click Activate Plugin.
  3. Go to Plugins and check that Video Gallery Block Pro is active. The free copy switches itself off, and that is normal.

Activate Your Licence

Your licence key unlocks updates and support. You enter it once on this site.

  1. Open Video Gallery in the WordPress menu and click Help & Demos.
  2. Click the Activation tab.
  3. Paste your key into the License key box.
  4. Click Activate License. The page then shows Activated.
Tip

Moving the licence to another site? Click Deactivate license here first. Then activate it on the new site.

Switch Blocks On or Off

Too many blocks in the inserter? Hide the ones you never use.

  1. Open Video Gallery, then Help & Demos, then click the Blocks tab.
  2. Turn the switch off on any block you want to hide.

Start From the Layout Picker

Not sure which block to use? The picker shows every layout as a card with a short note.

  1. Open Video Gallery in the WordPress menu and click Add New.
  2. Click a card under Select a block layout type.

The gallery gets a shortcode you can paste on any page. Ready to build? See Your First Pro Gallery.

Quick Start: Your First Pro Gallery

In about five minutes you will have a Pro block on a page with your own videos. We use the Video Slider because it is simple.

Step 1. Insert a Pro Block

Every Pro block lives in the inserter, under the Video Gallery category.

  1. Open a page in the editor and click the + button at the top left.
  2. Type Video Slider in the search box and click the block.

The block arrives with sample videos, so you can see the result at once.

Step 2. Add Your Videos

Replace the samples with your own. Every Pro block works this way. See Add Your Videos for the video sources.

  1. Select the block and open the Videos panel on the General tab. Click the first video to open it.
  2. Paste your link into the Video box. It can be a YouTube link, a Vimeo link or a file from your Media Library.
  3. Type a Title. Add a Poster image if you want your own thumbnail.
  4. Click Add New Video to add another one.
Tip

Delete the sample videos you do not replace. Anything you leave behind gets published too.

Step 3. Make It Fit Your Page

  • Open Options on the General tab for autoplay and video fit.
  • Open Layout Settings for gaps and sizes.
  • Use the Style tab for colours, fonts and borders.

Step 4. Preview and Publish

Click Preview, then check the page on a phone size too. When you like it, click Publish or Update.

Next, pick a block that fits your goal: Sliders, Grids, Playlists and Learning, Stories and Shorts or Business.

Sliders and Carousels

Show videos in a row that visitors swipe or click through. Three blocks do this, each in its own style.

Pick a Block

  • Video Slider for a simple slider of your own files.
  • Slider Autoplay Video for a slider that moves by itself.
  • Video Carousel Gallery for product videos with prices.

To add any of them, see Your First Pro Gallery.

Video Slider

A responsive slider for self-hosted videos. Visitors move with arrows, dots or a swipe.

You set up: a video, poster, title and rating for each slide. Then autoplay, video fit, the arrow buttons, the dots and the play button.

Slider Autoplay Video

A smooth slider that glides on its own. Use it for a hero strip near the top of a page.

You set up: the slide speed in milliseconds, loop on or off, the navigation arrows and which video starts in the middle. You can also open each video in a lightbox.

Video Carousel Gallery

A carousel of cards that you drag sideways. Each card can show a product with its price.

You set up: a video, poster, title, price and product link for each card. Then the currency symbol, loop, autoplay previews and the scrollbar. For a live store, see Product Videos with WooCommerce.

Grids and Walls

Show many videos at once, in a tidy or a bold layout. Six blocks cover every taste.

Pick a Block

  • Masonry Video Grid for tiles of different heights.
  • Video Justified Gallery for rows of equal height.
  • Video Bento Grid for a magazine style wall.
  • Video Showcase Wall for a streaming service look.
  • Lightbox Video Gallery for thumbnails that open in a popup.
  • Parallax Row Video Gallery for rows that move as you scroll.

Masonry Video Grid

Videos of different heights fit together with no gaps. It looks like a photo wall.

You set up: columns and gaps, play on hover, the video length label and the lightbox.

Video Justified Gallery

Mixed shapes sit in full width rows of equal height. Nothing gets stretched or cropped oddly.

You set up: the target row height, what the last row does, a hover preview and a load more button.

Video Bento Grid

Each video gets its own tile size, so one hero video can stand out. Tiles can preview on hover.

You set up: the span of each tile, columns, row height and how the grid folds on mobile.

Video Showcase Wall

Cards play a muted preview when you hover, then grow into a spotlight. Show them as a grid or as scrolling rows.

You set up: the layout, the preview delay, badges and a button on each card.

Lightbox Video Gallery

A clean grid of thumbnails. A click opens the video in a popup over the page.

You set up: columns and gaps, plus a source, title, subtitle and poster for each video.

Parallax Row Video Gallery

Rows of videos drift at different speeds as the visitor scrolls. It adds depth to a long page.

You set up: one group per row, with at least three videos in each. Then the direction, columns and the lightbox.

Playlists, Courses and Timelines

Use these when videos should be watched in a set order. Think lessons, episodes, steps and milestones.

Pick a Block

  • Video Playlist Gallery for a player with a list beside it.
  • Video Curriculum Accordion for course lessons.
  • Video Tabs Gallery for categories as tabs.
  • Video Timeline Gallery for dates along a line.
  • Video Scrollytelling Showcase for a story told while scrolling.
  • Video Podcast Episodes for a show page.

Video Playlist Gallery

A main player with a playlist beside it. Videos play one after another.

You set up: autoplay, loop, the up next toggle, a category and duration for each video, and the view count. See View Counts, Likes and Comments.

Video Curriculum Accordion

Course sections fold open to show their lessons. Lessons can carry a free preview badge.

You set up: sections, then lessons with a title, video, poster and duration. Then which section starts open, the progress bar and lightbox or inline play.

Video Tabs Gallery

Each tab is a category with its own videos. Switching is instant, with no page reload.

You set up: the tabs, the videos in each, the default tab, the tab style and position, and link anchors.

Video Timeline Gallery

Videos hang on dates along a line. Cards fade in as the visitor scrolls.

You set up: one milestone per date. Then the direction, alternating sides, grouping by year and the sort order.

Video Scrollytelling Showcase

A video stays pinned while the text beside it scrolls. The video swaps as each step appears.

You set up: the steps, each with a label, heading, text and video. Then the side the video sits on and the swap style.

Video Podcast Episodes

A show page with one featured episode, season tabs, show notes and a subscribe bar.

You set up: seasons and episodes, with guest, date, notes and chapter times. Then the featured episode and your Apple Podcasts, Spotify, YouTube and RSS links.

Video Workout Planner

A training program laid out by day. Each exercise card shows sets, reps and rest.

You set up: the program title and the training days. Then the exercises, with equipment, target muscle and difficulty, and the difficulty filter.

Stories, Shorts and Playful Layouts

Short, snappy video that feels like it belongs on a phone. These blocks also add movement to a quiet page.

Pick a Block

  • Video Stories for round rings that open a story.

Video Stories

A tray of round rings, like Instagram. A tap opens a fullscreen story that moves on by itself.

You set up: one story per ring, then the clips inside it. Then the ring shape and size, auto advance and a button on each clip.

Video Shorts Reel

A tall feed that snaps from one video to the next, like TikTok.

You set up: the shorts, then the layout (feed, side rail or grid) and the aspect ratio. Then the author, the action rail and the mute button.

Video Swipe Deck

A stack of cards. Visitors swipe or use the arrow keys, and the top card plays.

You set up: the cards, the stack size, swipe direction, loop and rewind. You can end with a closing card and a button.

Video Marquee Wall

Rows of video cards glide across the page in opposite directions. They play when hovered.

You set up: the rows, scroll speed, pause on hover, edge fade and what a click does.

Video Flip Cards Gallery

Each card turns over to play its video on the back. It adds a little fun to a grid.

You set up: columns, the flip trigger and axis, front text and back text, and a button on the back.

Video Expanding Panels

Side by side panels. Hover or click one and it opens to play while the others shrink.

You set up: the panels, trigger mode, panel height, which panel starts open and the mobile layout.

Video Wall with Floating Player

Click a tile and a small player pops up in a corner. It stays there while the visitor keeps scrolling.

You set up: the dock corner and width, drag and resize, category filters and a load more button.

Product Videos with WooCommerce

Turn product videos into a carousel that sells. Each card links to a real product in your store.

Before You Start

WooCommerce must be installed and active. If it is not, the editor shows a notice with a link to install it.

No store yet? You can still type a title, price and link by hand.

Link a Video to a Product

  1. Insert the Video Carousel Gallery block and open a video in the Videos panel.
  2. Switch on Use WooCommerce Product.
  3. Search for your product and choose it in Select Product.

The title, price, sale price and product image now come from the product.

Add a Product by Hand

Leave Use WooCommerce Product off. Then fill in Product Image, Title, Current Price, Original Price and Product URL.

With WooCommerce active, click Create Product to make a new store product from those details.

Fine Tune the Carousel

  • Currency Symbol sets the sign before prices.
  • Open in New Tab keeps the gallery open when the shop link is clicked.
  • Autoplay Previews plays each card muted as it scrolls into view.

View Counts, Likes and Comments

Let visitors see what is popular and join in. Two blocks do this: the Playlist Gallery and the Shorts Reel.

Show View Counts in a Playlist

The Video Playlist Gallery can show how many times a video was played on your site. Counts read like 12K views.

  1. Select the Video Playlist Gallery block and open Options on the General tab.
  2. Switch on View/Upload Date.

The count goes up each time someone plays a video. It starts from zero on your site.

Likes, Saves and Comments on Shorts

The Video Shorts Reel has an action rail beside each video. Visitors can like, comment and save.

  • Likes work for everyone, even without an account.
  • Comments and saves need a logged in visitor.

Switch the rail on with Show Action Rail. Switch the save button on with its own setting in the same block.

Show a Visitor Their Saved Videos

Paste the shortcode [vsr_saved_videos] on any page. A logged in visitor sees their saved videos as a grid.

Moderate Comments

You stay in charge of what gets posted. Open Video Gallery and click Reel Moderation.

Each video shows its likes and comments. Delete one comment, delete all comments, or reset the likes.

Troubleshooting

Something looks wrong? Most problems have a quick fix. Find the one that matches what you see.

Pro Blocks Look Locked After I Upgraded

You see a notice or locked controls even though you bought Pro. The licence is probably not active yet.

  • Open Video Gallery, then Help & Demos, then Activation. It should say Activated. See Activate Your Licence.
  • Check that Video Gallery Block Pro is the active plugin on the Plugins page.
  • Update to the latest version, then reload the editor and the page.

The Licence Will Not Activate

The Activation tab shows a message. Read it, because it says what is wrong.

  • Invalid or not found: copy the key again and check for extra spaces.
  • Expired: renew the licence, then try again.
  • No activations left: click Deactivate license on a site you no longer use, or buy a bigger plan.

A Block Is Missing from the Inserter

You search for a Pro block and nothing appears. It may be switched off.

Open Video Gallery, then Help & Demos, then Blocks. Turn its switch back on, then reload the editor. See Switch Blocks On or Off.

A Card Shows a Blank Thumbnail

Some links give no picture of their own. Open the video and add a Poster image. It is used as the thumbnail everywhere in that block.

A Video Will Not Start with Sound

Browsers block autoplay with sound until the visitor clicks. This is a browser rule, not a fault.

Keep autoplay muted, or let the visitor press play. Settings such as Mute Autoplay or Start Muted help here.

A Story Moves On Too Early or Too Late

YouTube and Vimeo clips cannot tell the block when they end. Open the clip in Video Stories and set its Duration (s) to the real length.

The Product List Is Empty

The WooCommerce list needs WooCommerce. Install and activate it, then reload the editor. See Before You Start.

Also check that you have at least one published product.

Visitors Cannot Save or Comment

Saving and commenting on the Shorts Reel need a logged in visitor. Likes work for everyone.

Add a login link near the block, or let visitors register. See Likes, Saves and Comments on Shorts.
