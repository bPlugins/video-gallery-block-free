Video Gallery Block: Introduction

You have a pile of YouTube and Vimeo links and no tidy place to show them. This plugin turns them into one gallery that plays in a popup.

You build it in the block editor. No code, no API key.

What You Get for Free

  • YouTube, Vimeo and Facebook videos in one gallery.
  • Albums and filter buttons so visitors find a topic fast.
  • Bulk Import. Paste a list of links and the gallery fills itself.
  • A lightbox that plays each video in a popup.
  • Privacy and Google Analytics options.

Where to Go Next

Start with the Quick Start. You will publish a gallery in about five minutes. Not installed yet? See Installation and Activation.

Installation and Activation

You want the plugin on your site without any fuss. It takes about a minute, with no code and no account.

Install from your dashboard

WordPress downloads the plugin for you.

  1. Open Plugins in the left menu, then click Add Plugin (the menu item or the button at the top).
  2. Type Video Gallery Block in the Search plugins box.
  3. Find the plugin by bPlugins and click Install Now.
  4. Click Activate when the button changes.

Check that it worked

A new Video Gallery item appears in your left menu. The plugin is ready.

Tip

Have a ZIP file instead? Choose Plugins, Add New Plugin, then Upload Plugin.

Quick Start: Your First Gallery

You have a page and a few videos, and you want a gallery people actually click. Here is your first one.

Add the block to a page

  1. Go to Pages in the left menu, then click Add Page.
  2. The editor opens with an empty page. Click the + button at the top left.
  3. Type Video Gallery Block in the search box.
  4. Click Video Gallery Block to add it to the page.
  5. In the right sidebar, open the General tab, then the Videos panel.
  6. Paste a YouTube or Vimeo link into the Video url box.
  7. Click Publish, or Update if the page is already live.

The title and thumbnail fill in by themselves.

Or start from a ready made pattern

A pattern is a finished section. You only swap in your own videos.

  1. Click the + button at the top, then open the Patterns tab.
  2. Pick the Video Galleries category.
  3. Pick a pattern. Try Video gallery with album filters.
  4. Click Edit pattern, then click the gallery. Replace the sample videos in the Videos panel with your own.

The five patterns

  • Video gallery with album filters: three columns and three albums.
  • Course video library: four columns, filtered by module.
  • Product demo showcase: three large tiles.
  • Customer video stories: video testimonials with the speaker named.
  • Portfolio showreel wall: four tight columns, no captions.
Tip

Sample videos are placeholders. Replace each one before you publish.

Add Videos

You found the video you want on your page. Here is how to add it, with its thumbnail and caption.

Add one video

  1. Select the gallery, then open the General tab in the right sidebar.
  2. Click Add New Video. There are two ways. The first is the plus button in the block toolbar.

    The second is the Add New Video button at the bottom of the Videos panel.

  3. Paste the link into the Video url box inside the Videos panel.

Works with YouTube, Vimeo and Facebook links.

Set the thumbnail and caption

For YouTube and Vimeo, these fill in for you. You can change them any time.

  1. Click the Poster url box, then paste an image link or choose an image from your Media Library.
  2. Type your own text in Caption:.
Tip

Facebook videos do not fill in a title or poster. Add them by hand.

Thumbnail pictures stay on your site

Thumbnails are saved to your Media Library once, so your pages stay quick.

Bulk Import

Twenty videos means twenty trips to the same box. Paste all the links at once instead.

Import many videos

  1. Select the gallery and open the General tab.
  2. Open the Import Videos panel.
  3. Paste your links into Video links, one link on each line.
  4. Click Import videos.

A message tells you how many videos were added and how many got a title and a poster.

Good to know

  • Links already in the gallery are skipped.
  • Lines that are not links are skipped.
  • If every link is a YouTube Short, the thumbnail shape switches to 9:16.

Albums, Filter Bar and Lightbox

Your tutorials, stories and demos are all mixed together. Albums sort them, so visitors pick the group they want.

Create an album

  1. Open the General tab, then the Add or Remove Albums panel.
  2. Click Add New Album.
  3. Type a name in the new box.

To delete an album, click the small cross next to its name.

Put a video in an album

  1. Open a video in the Videos panel.
  2. Tick the album under Select Albums:. A video can sit in more than one.

Next, see Filter Bar and Lightbox.

Filter Bar and Lightbox

Visitors want one topic, not forty videos. The filter bar finds it fast and the lightbox plays it without leaving the page.

Show or hide the filter bar

  1. Open the General tab, then the Filter panel.
  2. Switch Show Filter Bar on or off.
  3. Change the text in Common Filter. It is the button that shows every video. The default is All Videos.

The bar only shows when the gallery has at least one album.

Change the button colors

  1. Open the Style tab, then the Filter panel.
  2. Set Button Colors for the normal look and Button Hover/Active Colors for the chosen button.
  3. Change the font and size with Button Typography:.

The lightbox

A click on a thumbnail opens the video in a popup. There is nothing to switch on. Small thumbnails show where you are, arrows move between videos and a cross closes it.

Style the caption inside the lightbox

  1. Open the Style tab, then the LightBox Caption panel.
  2. Change Caption Typography:.
  3. Change Caption Colors:.
  4. Change Caption Padding:.

The play icon

A play icon on every thumbnail shows it is a video.

  1. Open the General tab, then the Options panel.
  2. Switch Show Play Icon on Thumbnail on or off.
  3. Open the Style tab, then the Item panel.
  4. Change Play Icon Colors:, then Play Icon Size:.
  5. Change Play Icon Hover Zoom:.

Reuse a Gallery with a Shortcode

You built one great gallery and now want it on five pages. Save it once and paste one short line wherever you need it.

Create a saved gallery

  1. Open Video Gallery in the left menu, then click Add New Video Gallery.
  2. Add your videos in the block, then click Publish.
  3. Click the copy button next to the shortcode bar at the top of the editor.

Paste it on a page

  1. Edit any page, click the + button, and type Shortcode in the search box.
  2. Click the Shortcode block to add it to the page.
  3. The block appears with an empty box that says Write shortcode here. Click inside that box.
  4. Paste the line, for example [video_gallery id=12]. Your number will differ.
  5. Click Publish.
  6. Click View Page. It is in the notice at the bottom of the screen, and also as an icon in the top bar.
  7. The page opens in a new tab. The gallery now shows where you pasted the line.
Tip

The editor only shows the shortcode as text. The gallery appears on the live page. You can also copy it from the Shortcode column on the Video Gallery list page.

Layout and Styling

Phones and desktops need different layouts. This panel sets the columns, gaps and sizes for each.

Columns for each device

Phones need fewer columns than desktops, so set each device on its own.

  1. Open the General tab, then the Layout Settings panel.
  2. Pick Desktop, Tablet or Mobile with the device icon next to Columns:.
  3. Drag the slider to choose 1 to 6 columns.

Space between tiles

  1. Pick a device, then type a number in Column Gap:. This is the space left and right.
  2. Type a number in Row Gap:. This is the space above and below.

Place the last row

If the last row is not full, you choose where its tiles sit.

  1. Choose Left, Center or Right under Row Alignment:.

Sort order

  1. Choose Manual, Newest First or Oldest First under Sort Order:.

Manual keeps the order you added them in.

Show a few, then Load More

A big gallery can feel heavy. Show a few videos first and let visitors ask for more.

  1. Type a number in Videos Per Page:. Zero shows every video.
  2. Change Load More Button Text: for your own wording. It appears once a number is set.

Thumbnail shape

The shape keeps every tile the same on all screens. Pick 9:16 for tall Shorts and Reels.

  1. Open the Style tab, then the Item panel.
  2. Choose Fixed, 16:9, 4:3, 1:1 or 9:16 under Thumbnail Shape:.
  3. With Fixed, type a number in the Height: box that appears.

Show or hide YouTube Shorts

  1. Open the General tab, then the Options panel.
  2. Choose All Videos, Hide Shorts or Only Shorts under YouTube Shorts:.

Poster fit and captions on thumbnails

  1. In the Options panel, choose Contain, Cover, Fill or None under Poster Fit:. Cover fills the tile fully.
  2. Switch Show Caption on Thumbnail on to place each caption over its tile.

Styling

Your brand has its own colors and feel. Here is where you make the gallery match it.

Style the whole gallery

The gallery is the box around all your tiles.

  1. Open the Style tab, then the Gallery panel.
  2. Pick a color or image under Background:.
  3. Set the space inside the box with Padding:.
  4. Add an outline and round the corners with Border:.
  5. Add a soft drop shadow with Shadow:.

Style each tile

These apply to every thumbnail.

  1. Open the Item panel in the Style tab.
  2. Round the corners or add an outline with Item Border:.
  3. Add a shadow under each tile with Item Shadow:.

Style the captions

  1. In the Item panel, set the font with Caption Typography:.
  2. Set text and background colors with Caption Colors:.
  3. Set the space around the text with Caption Padding:.
Tip

Change one thing at a time. The editor updates as you go.

Privacy, Analytics and SEO

Some visitors need to agree before a video loads, and you may want to know which videos get played. Two optional switches cover both.

Ask before a video loads

YouTube, Vimeo and Facebook can set cookies when a player loads. This switch asks the visitor first.

  1. Open the General tab, then the Options panel.
  2. Switch Require Consent Before Playing on.

Visitors see a notice that names the video host. They click Play Video to continue or Cancel to stop. The browser remembers their answer.

Count video plays in Google Analytics

See which videos people really watch. Google Analytics 4 must already be on your site, through Site Kit, a pasted Google tag or Google Tag Manager.

  1. In the Options panel, switch Send Video Play Events to Google Analytics on.

The gallery then sends these events, each with the video title, the host and the link:

  • video_start when a visitor opens a video.
  • video_play when they press play.
  • video_watch_progress at 25, 50 and 75 percent.
  • video_watch_complete when the video ends.

To check it, open Google Analytics, go to Reports > Realtime, and play a video on your site in a private window.

Tip

Facebook videos only send video_start, because Facebook does not share play details. Using Google Tag Manager? Make a Custom Event trigger for each event name.

Video SEO

You want Google to find your videos. The plugin tells search engines each video's title, thumbnail and link, so your gallery can show up as a video result.

Turn it on or off

It is on by default. Turn it off only if your SEO plugin already adds this.

  1. Open the General tab, then the Options panel.
  2. Switch Video SEO markup on or off.

Help it work better

  1. Give every video a clear caption.
  2. Give every video a thumbnail.
  3. Use real video links, not links to other pages.

Dashboard and Help Page

Looking for demos and settings? They live on one help page in your admin.

Open the page

  1. Click Video Gallery in the left menu.
  2. Click Help & Demos.

The tabs at the top are Welcome, Blocks, Demos, Pricing, Feature Comparison and Settings. On a narrow screen they sit behind the menu button.

Keep or delete your data on uninstall

By default your galleries stay if you remove the plugin. You can change that.

  1. Open the Settings tab.
  2. Switch the toggle under Delete plugin data on uninstall. Confirm the warning.
Warning

With this on, deleting the plugin removes all your saved galleries for good. Turning the plugin off deletes nothing.

Pro Overview and Quick Start

One gallery layout is not enough for every page. Pro adds 31 ready made layouts, so you pick one, add your videos and publish.

What You Get

  • 31 more blocks, each built for one job.
  • WooCommerce product videos in the Video Carousel Gallery.

Install Pro Next to Free

You do not need to remove anything. Pro carries its own copy of the Video Gallery block, so your saved galleries keep working.

  1. Open Plugins and click Add Plugin.
  2. Click Upload Plugin.
  3. Click Choose File, pick the Pro zip file from your purchase, then click Install Now.
  4. Click Activate Plugin.
  5. Go to Plugins and check that Video Gallery Block Pro is active. The free copy switches itself off. That is normal.

Activate Your Licence

Your licence key unlocks updates and support. You enter it once on this site.

  1. Open Video Gallery and click Help & Demos.
  2. Click the Activation tab.
  3. Paste your key into the License key box.
  4. Click Activate License. The page then shows Activated.
Tip

Moving to another site? Click Deactivate license here first.

Switch Blocks On or Off

Too many blocks in the inserter? Hide the ones you never use.

  1. Open Video Gallery and click Help & Demos.
  2. Click the Blocks tab.
  3. Turn the switch off on any block you want to hide.

Start From the Layout Picker

  1. Open Video Gallery and click Add New Video Gallery.

Quick Start: Your First Pro Gallery

You bought Pro and want to see it work. In about five minutes you will have a Video Slider on a page with your own videos.

Step 1. Insert a Pro Block

Every Pro block lives in the inserter, under the Video Gallery category.

  1. Open a page in the editor and click the + button at the top left.
  2. Type Video Slider in the search box and click the block.

The block arrives with sample videos, so you see a result at once.

Step 2. Add Your Videos

Replace the samples with your own. See Add Your Videos for the video sources.

  1. Click the block to select it, then open the General tab.
  2. Open the Videos panel and click the first video.
  3. Paste your link into the Video box. It can be a YouTube link, a Vimeo link or a file from your Media Library.
  4. Type a Title. Add a Poster image if you want your own thumbnail.
  5. Click Add New Video to add another one.
Tip

Delete the samples you do not replace. Anything left behind gets published.

Step 3. Make It Fit Your Page

  1. Open Options on the General tab for autoplay and video fit.
  2. Open Layout Settings for gaps and sizes.
  3. Open the Style tab for colours, fonts and borders.

Step 4. Preview and Publish

  1. Click Preview and check the page at phone size too.
  2. Click Publish or Update.

Next, pick a block: Sliders, Grids, Playlists and Learning, Stories and Shorts or Business.

Product Videos, Likes and Comments

Your product videos should sell, not just play. Each card in this carousel links to a real product in your store.

Before You Start

WooCommerce must be installed and active. If it is not, the editor shows a notice with an install link. No store yet? You can still type a title, price and link by hand.

Link a Video to a Product

  1. Insert the Video Carousel Gallery block and open a video in the Videos panel.
  2. Switch on Use WooCommerce Product.
  3. Search for your product and choose it in Select Product.

The title, price, sale price and image now come from the product.

Add a Product by Hand

  1. Leave Use WooCommerce Product off.
  2. Add the Product Image and the Title.
  3. Type the Current Price and the Original Price.
  4. Paste the Product URL.
  5. With WooCommerce active, click Create Product to make a store product from those details.

Fine Tune the Carousel

  1. Type the sign before prices in Currency Symbol.
  2. Switch on Open in New Tab to keep the gallery open when the shop link is clicked.
  3. Switch on Autoplay Previews to play each card muted as it scrolls into view.

View Counts, Likes and Comments

You want visitors to see what is popular and join in. The Playlist Gallery and the Shorts Reel do this.

Show View Counts in a Playlist

Counts read like 12K views. They start from zero on your site and go up with each play.

  1. Click the Video Playlist Gallery block to select it.
  2. Open Options on the General tab.
  3. Switch on View/Upload Date.

Likes, Saves and Comments on Shorts

Likes work for everyone. Comments and saves need a logged in visitor.

  1. Select the Video Shorts Reel block.
  2. Switch on Show Action Rail.

The save bookmark needs no switch. Logged in visitors see it on the card automatically. You can restyle it in the Saved Button panel on the Style tab.

Show a Visitor Their Saved Videos

Paste the shortcode [vsr_saved_videos] on any page. A logged in visitor sees their saved videos as a grid.

Moderate Comments

  1. Open Video Gallery and click Reel Moderation.
  2. Delete one comment, delete all comments, or reset the likes.

Troubleshooting

Something looks off? Most problems have a quick fix. Find yours below.

Pro Blocks Look Locked After I Upgraded

The licence is probably not active. Open Video Gallery, Help & Demos, then Activation, and check it says Activated. See Activate Your Licence.

The Licence Will Not Activate

Read the message on the Activation tab. It says what is wrong, such as a mistyped key, an expired licence or no activations left.

A Block Is Missing from the Inserter

It may be switched off. Turn it back on in the Blocks tab and reload the editor. See Switch Blocks On or Off.

A Card Shows a Blank Thumbnail

Open the video and add a Poster image. It becomes the thumbnail.

A Video Will Not Start with Sound

Browsers block autoplay with sound until the visitor clicks. Keep autoplay muted, or let the visitor press play.
