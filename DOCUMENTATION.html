Getting Started with Video Gallery Block

Welcome to the Video Gallery Block user guide. This guide shows you how to add videos to your WordPress site, organize them into albums, choose a layout, change the design and show the same gallery on other pages.

You do not need coding skills or a technical background. Follow the steps in order and you can build your first video gallery without learning WordPress developer terms.

Before You Begin

Make sure you have the following:

  • A WordPress website where you can install plugins. It needs WordPress 6.5 or newer, PHP 7.4 or newer and the block editor, which is the default editor. Most hosts already meet this.
  • Administrator access so you can install and activate Video Gallery Block.
  • At least one video link from YouTube, Vimeo or Facebook that you want to show on your site.
  • A thumbnail image if you want to use your own. This is optional, because YouTube and Vimeo supply one automatically.

After installation, start with Create Your First Video Gallery. The first setup will teach you the basic workflow before you explore the extra features. If you later want more layouts, see Set Up Video Gallery Pro.

Installation and Activation

Before you build a gallery, make sure the plugin is available in your WordPress editor. This is a one-time setup; after activation, you can move straight to creating your first gallery.

Install the Plugin

Use WordPress's built-in plugin installer; it downloads and prepares the plugin for you.

  1. Open Plugins in the left menu, then click Add Plugin (the menu item or the button at the top).
  2. Type Video Gallery Block in the Search plugins box.
  3. Find the plugin by bPlugins and click Install Now.
  4. Click Activate when the button changes.

Make Sure the Plugin Is Active

After activation, look for Video Gallery in the left side of your WordPress dashboard. Seeing that menu is the sign that setup is complete.

Tip

If you received a ZIP file instead, open Plugins > Add New Plugin > Upload Plugin, choose the file and install it.

Now turn the empty page into a working gallery: Create Your First Video Gallery.

Create Your First Video Gallery

The quickest way to learn the block is to publish a simple gallery first. Once you have seen it on your page, you can add more videos and explore the settings without guessing what each option changes.

Add the Video Gallery Block to a Page

  1. Go to Pages in the left menu, then click Add Page (the menu item or the button at the top).
  2. The editor opens with an empty page. Click the + button at the top left.
  3. Type Video Gallery Block in the search box.
  4. Click Video Gallery Block to add it to the page.
  5. In the right sidebar, open the General tab, then the Videos panel. It sits right under the Video source panel.
  6. Paste a YouTube or Vimeo link into the Video box.
  7. Click Publish, or Update if the page is already live.

For YouTube and Vimeo, the plugin usually fills in the title and thumbnail for you, so you can see the gallery take shape with very little setup. Add more videos the same way and each one becomes a tile in the gallery.

Your first gallery is live. That is the first milestone. Next, add the videos you actually want visitors to watch; you can organize and style the gallery once the content is in place.

Start with a Ready-Made Pattern (Optional)

Prefer a ready-made design? A pattern is a layout you can insert instead of starting from a blank page. If you already published the simple gallery above, treat these steps as an alternative way to start another gallery.

  1. Click the + button at the top, then open the Patterns tab.
  2. Pick the Video Galleries category.
  3. Pick a pattern. Try Video gallery with album filters.
  4. Click Edit pattern, then click the gallery. Replace the sample videos in the Videos panel with your own.

Available Gallery Patterns

  • Video gallery with album filters: three columns and three albums.
  • Course video library: four columns, filtered by module.
  • Product demo showcase: three large tiles.
  • Customer video stories: video testimonials with the speaker named.
  • Portfolio showreel wall: four tight columns, no captions.
Tip

The sample videos are only placeholders. Replace every one you want to keep in the layout before publishing, or visitors may see the samples.

Now fill the gallery with your own content: Add Videos to Your Gallery.

Add Videos to Your Gallery

Your first gallery gives you the starting point. Now add the videos people came to see: add them one at a time when you want control, or use Bulk Import when you already have a list of links.

Add One Video

  1. Select the gallery, then open the General tab in the right sidebar.
  2. To add a video, click the plus (+) button in the block toolbar.

    Or click Add New Video at the bottom of the Videos panel.

  3. Paste the link into the Video box of the new video in the Videos panel. You can also upload a video file there.

You can use YouTube, Vimeo or Facebook links, including Facebook Reels. The thumbnail is the preview image people see before playing; the title is the text shown with it.

Change the Thumbnail and Title

YouTube and Vimeo usually supply a title and thumbnail automatically. Keep them for speed, or replace them when you want a more consistent look across your gallery.

  1. Click the Thumbnail box, then paste an image link, or upload or choose an image from your Media Library.
  2. Type your own text in Title.
Tip

Facebook links do not fill in the title or poster automatically, so add those details yourself.

Where the Thumbnail Is Saved

Downloaded thumbnails are saved in your WordPress Media Library, the same place you manage the other images on your site.

Add Many Videos from the Media Library

Do you have video files on your own site? Add all of them in one go, instead of pasting one link at a time.

  1. Select the gallery, open the General tab, then open the Import videos panel.
  2. Click Add from Media Library.
  3. Tick as many videos as you like, or upload new ones in the same window, then confirm. Every video becomes a tile, with its name as the title.
Tip

You can also drag video files from your computer straight onto the gallery in the editor. A box that says Drop videos to add them shows up while you drag. YouTube and Vimeo links work the same way: drag the link from the address bar of another browser tab and drop it on the gallery.

Change the Order of Your Videos

The tiles show up in the order of your list. Move a video when you want it first, or last.

  1. Hold the mouse over a tile in the editor. A small bar shows up on the tile. Drag the dotted handle to a new place, or click an arrow to move the tile one step.
  2. Prefer the sidebar? Open the Videos panel, click a video, then click the Move earlier or Move later button at the top of the panel.

To sort by date instead, see Choose the Video Order.

Add Many Videos with Bulk Import

Already have a list of links? Bulk Import saves you from repeating the same add-video steps for every item.

Import Multiple Video Links

  1. Select the gallery and open the General tab.
  2. Open the Import videos panel, just under the Videos panel.
  3. Paste your links into Video links, one link on each line.
  4. Click Import videos.

After the import, a message reports how many videos were added and how many received a title and thumbnail.

Good to know

  • Links already in the gallery are skipped.
  • Lines that are not links are skipped.
  • If the gallery was empty, no thumbnail shape was chosen yet and every link is a YouTube Short, Thumbnail shape in the Layout panel switches to 9:16.
Tip

Posting new videos to YouTube or Vimeo every week? Instead of adding each one here, let the gallery follow your channel or playlist. See Show a YouTube or Vimeo Channel Automatically.

Once the gallery has several videos, help visitors find the right one by grouping related clips into albums and adding filters. If you would rather fill the gallery from a channel, continue to Show a YouTube or Vimeo Channel Automatically first.

YouTube & Vimeo Channel Feeds

Post new videos often? Let the gallery follow your channel or playlist. Paste one link, and every new upload shows up by itself.

This is called a feed. Vimeo links work at once. YouTube links need a free key from Google, which you make one time.

Switch the Gallery to a Channel or Playlist

  1. Select the gallery. On the General tab, open Video source.
  2. Choose YouTube / Vimeo.
  3. Paste your link into the YouTube or Vimeo link box.
  4. Click Show videos. For YouTube, connect it first (see below).

When the videos load, step 1 turns green.

Tip

Which Links You Can Use

  • YouTube channel: the channel's address, such as youtube.com/@name.
  • YouTube playlist: a link with list=. It must not be private.
  • Vimeo profile: such as vimeo.com/name.
  • Vimeo channel or group: such as vimeo.com/channels/name or vimeo.com/groups/name.
  • Vimeo showcase: vimeo.com/showcase/ and a number.

A link to one video does not work. A feed needs a channel or playlist.

Only public videos are shown.

Connect YouTube with a Free Key (One Time)

An API key is a free code from Google. It lets your site ask YouTube for a channel's videos. You make it once, and it works for every gallery. Vimeo does not need a key. Follow the steps below.

  1. Click Connect YouTube.
  2. Click Open Google Cloud and sign in with your Google account.
  3. At the top, click the project name (or Select a project), then New project.
  4. Type any name and click Create.
  5. When the message appears, click Select project. (Missed it? Click the bell icon.)
  6. Back in the editor, click Open the API page.
  7. Click Enable.
  8. In the left menu, click Credentials.
  9. At the top, click Create credentials, then click API key.
  10. Under Select API restrictions, tick YouTube Data API v3 and click OK.
  11. Click Create.
  12. Copy the key. It starts with AIza.
  13. In the editor, paste the key into YouTube API key and click Save key.

The key is free. Keep it private, like a password.

When the key works, step 2 turns green and says Connected. Your videos load.

  • To use another key later, open step 2 and click Change key.

Choose How Many Videos and How Often to Check

  1. Open How many & how often. Drag Number of videos (up to 50, newest first).
  2. Optional: type a number in Show first. The rest wait behind a Load More button.
  3. Under Check for new videos, pick how often: 1 h, 6 h, 12 h or Daily.
  4. Click Check now to get the newest videos right away.

Hide Shorts or Show Only Shorts

Show all videos, hide Shorts, or show only Shorts.

  1. In How many & how often, choose under YouTube Shorts.

Go Back to Your Own Videos

Choose My videos under Where do the videos come from?. Your own list is still there. Nothing was deleted.

Copy a Feed to Your Own List

Want to rename, remove or reorder feed videos? Copy them to your own list first.

  1. In Video source, click Copy these videos to My videos.
  2. Made a mistake? Click Undo in the message.

The copied videos are now yours. They no longer update by themselves.

Good to Know

  • New videos are not instant. The gallery checks at the time you chose. This keeps your page fast.
  • The gallery never goes empty. If YouTube cannot be reached, it keeps the last videos it had.
  • Using a cache plugin? Visitors may see new videos only after the cache refreshes.

Get Told When a Feed Stops Updating

If a feed keeps failing, a red notice appears at the top of the Dashboard. It says what went wrong, for example the key is not valid. It goes away by itself when the feed works again.

  • Click Hide for a day to hide it for 24 hours.
  • Click Email me about feed problems to get an email too.
Tip

With Pro, a feed can mix several channels, show live streams and work in 20 other blocks. See Pro YouTube & Vimeo Feeds.

Next: Customize Gallery Layout & Style.

Pro YouTube & Vimeo Feeds

The free gallery follows one channel. With Pro, a feed can mix several channels, show live streams, filter by title, and work in 20 other blocks.

Set up the feed first, as in Show a YouTube or Vimeo Channel Automatically. With Pro you get two more optional steps: Keywords and Live streams.

Choose What the Feed Shows

  1. Open Video source and choose YouTube / Vimeo.
  2. Pick a Feed type card: Channel, Live, Search or Videos.
  3. Fill in the field below, then click Show videos.
  • Channel: a channel or playlist link.
  • Live: a channel's live streams.
  • Search: type words, for example wordpress tutorial. You get the top 50 videos.
  • Videos: paste up to 50 video links, one per line.

Search Feeds Use More Quota

Google gives your key 10,000 free units a day. A search check costs 100 units. So for search feeds, keep Check for new videos at a few hours or Daily. Do not use 15 min.

Combine Several Channels or Playlists

One gallery can follow up to 10 links. You can mix YouTube and Vimeo. Videos are shown together, newest first.

  1. Under the first link, find More channels or playlists.
  2. Paste another link and click Add link.

Show More Videos, or All of Them

  • Number of videos goes up to 1,000.
  • Turn on All videos to show every video. A Load More button shows the rest.

Show or Hide Videos by Title Words

Use the Keywords step when a channel posts more than you want to show.

  1. Type words into Only show videos whose title has, for example tutorial, review.
  2. Type words into Hide videos whose title has, for example sponsored, trailer.

Capital letters do not matter. Leave both boxes empty to show every video.

Show Live Streams

The Live streams step works with YouTube.

Under When the channel is live, choose one:

  • Player on top (default): the live stream plays in a big player above the gallery.
  • First in gallery: the live stream moves to the front with a LIVE badge.
  • Normal order: no change.

Also in this step:

  • Live stream link: paste a stream that is not in your list.
  • Hide upcoming streams: hides streams that have not started.

Check for New Videos Every 15 Minutes

Pro adds a 15 min button to Check for new videos. Use it for a channel that posts or goes live often.

Use a Feed in Other Pro Blocks

20 Pro blocks can use a feed too. They have the same Video source panel.

  1. Select the block. On the General tab, open Video source.
  2. Choose YouTube / Vimeo, paste your link and click Show videos.
  3. In How many & how often, set how many videos and how often to check.

Blocks with a feed: Lightbox Video Gallery, Masonry Video Grid, Parallax Row Video Gallery, Slider Autoplay Video, Video Bento Grid, Video Carousel Gallery, Video Curriculum Accordion, Video Expanding Panels, Video Flip Cards Gallery, Video Justified Gallery, Video Marquee Wall, Video Wall with Floating Player, Video Playlist Gallery, Video Podcast Episodes, Video Shorts Reel, Video Showcase Wall, Video Slider, Video Swipe Deck, Video Tabs Gallery and Video Timeline Gallery.

  • Live streams: use the Show live streams first switch.
  • Shorts: use the YouTube Shorts buttons.
  • Video Shorts Reel shows only Shorts. Choose All Videos to add normal videos.
  • Video Curriculum Accordion: each link is one section. Add more under More playlists — each one is a section. Set Videos per section.
  • Video Tabs Gallery: each link is one tab. Add more under More playlists — each one is a tab. Set Videos per tab.
  • Slider Autoplay Video: in Layout, switch on Fill the card with YouTube / Vimeo to remove dark bands.

Video Stories, Video Comparison Gallery and Video Map Gallery have no feed.

Albums, Filters & Lightbox

A few videos are easy to browse. As the gallery grows, visitors need a quick way to find a topic instead of scanning every thumbnail. Start by grouping related clips into albums, then let visitors filter by topic.

An album is a group of related videos, such as Tutorials, Products or Customer Stories. The filter bar displays the album names as buttons visitors can choose.

Create an Album

  1. Open the General tab, then the Albums & filter bar panel, under Import videos.
  2. Click Add album.
  3. Type a name in the new box, labelled Album 1.

To remove an album you no longer need, click the small cross (Remove this album) next to its name.

Add a Video to an Album

  1. Open a video in the Videos panel.
  2. Tick the boxes for the albums you want under Albums, below the video's Title field.

A video can belong to more than one album, so the same clip can appear under more than one topic.

Let Visitors Filter and Watch Videos

Once the albums are assigned, visitors can choose a topic from the filter bar. When they click a thumbnail, the lightbox opens the video in a popup so they can watch without leaving the page.

Show or Hide the Filter Bar

The filter bar settings are in the same panel as the albums. They appear under a Filter bar heading once the gallery has at least one album.

  1. Open the General tab, then the Albums & filter bar panel, and scroll to Filter bar under the album list.
  2. Switch Show filter bar on or off.
  3. Change the text in "All" button label. It is the button that shows every video. The default is All Videos. Leave it empty to hide the filter bar.

The filter bar appears only when the gallery has at least one album. If you cannot see it, check that an album exists and that a video has been assigned to it.

Change the Filter Button Style

  1. Open the Style tab, then the Album filter bar panel. It is listed only while the filter bar is shown.
  2. Set Button colors for the normal look and Hover & active colors for the chosen button.
  3. Change the font and size with Button typography.

How the Lightbox Works

No separate setup is needed for the lightbox. Visitors can move between videos with the arrows or close the popup with the cross.

Play Videos in Their Tiles Instead

By default, a click opens the video in the lightbox. Choose Play in place if you would rather keep visitors in the gallery: the video plays right inside its own tile, and the rest of the page stays as it is.

  1. Open the General tab, then the Playback panel.
  2. Under When a video is clicked, choose Play in place. Choose Lightbox to go back to the popup.
  • Only one video plays at a time.
  • Set Thumbnail shape in the Layout panel to 16:9, or 9:16 for Shorts, so the video fills its tile. See Choose the Thumbnail Shape.
  • A tile that has only a thumbnail image, or a link that is not a video, still opens in the lightbox.
  • Settings that only affect the popup, such as the Lightbox caption panel in the Style tab and Popup width as aspect ratio in Playback, are hidden while Play in place is chosen.

Style the Lightbox Caption

  1. Open the Style tab, then the Lightbox caption panel, the last one in the list.
  2. Change Typography.
  3. Change Colors.
  4. Change Padding.

Show or Style the Play Icon

The play icon is a visual cue that a tile contains a video. Keep it visible if visitors might mistake the thumbnail for a regular image, or adjust its size and color to match your design.

  1. Open the General tab, then the Display panel.
  2. Switch Show play icon on or off.
  3. Open the Style tab, then the Play icon panel. It is listed only while Show play icon is on.
  4. Change Colors, then Size (30 to 100 px).
  5. Change Hover zoom: how much the icon grows when the pointer is on it. Set it to 1 to turn the zoom off.

Your videos are now easier to browse. If you want to reuse this same gallery on another page, continue to Show a Gallery Anywhere with Shortcodes.

Customize Layout & Style

Make the gallery look good on a computer, a tablet and a phone.

Adjust Columns for Desktop, Tablet and Mobile

Use more columns on a computer and fewer on a phone.

  1. On the General tab, open Layout.
  2. Pick a device with the icon next to Columns.
  3. Drag the slider to choose 1 to 6 columns.

Adjust the Space Between Videos

Set the space between videos in Layout, under Gaps.

  1. Type a number in Between columns (left and right).
  2. Type a number in Between rows (above and below).

Align the Last Row

If the last row has fewer videos, choose where they sit.

  1. In Layout, choose Left, Center or Right under Row alignment.

Match the Popup to the Video Shape

Let the popup follow each video's shape.

  1. On the General tab, open Playback.
  2. Switch on Popup width as aspect ratio.

Choose the Video Order

  1. In Layout, choose Manual, Newest or Oldest under Sort order.

Manual keeps your own order. For a channel, it reads Feed order.

Show Fewer Videos First with Load More

For a long gallery, show a few videos first. A Load More button shows the rest.

  1. Open Display. Under Videos shown, type a number in Show first. Zero shows all.
  2. Change Load more button text if you like.

For a channel, find these in How many & how often. See Choose How Many Videos and How Often to Check.

Choose the Thumbnail Shape

Pick a shape for the thumbnails. Use 9:16 for Shorts.

  1. In Layout, find Thumbnails.
  2. Choose Fixed, 16:9, 4:3, 1:1 or 9:16 under Thumbnail shape.
  3. With Fixed, type a Height.

Show All Videos or Only YouTube Shorts

This shows when the gallery has a YouTube video.

  1. On the General tab, open Display.
  2. Under Videos shown, choose All, Hide or Only under YouTube Shorts.

Hide leaves Shorts out. Only shows only Shorts. For a channel, see Hide Shorts or Show Only Shorts.

Control Thumbnail Fit and Titles

  1. In Layout, choose Cover, Contain, Fill or None under Thumbnail fit.
  2. In Display, switch Show title on.

Show the Channel Name

Show the channel name under each title. This works for channel galleries only.

  1. Open the General tab, then the Display panel.
  2. Switch Show channel name on.

Change Colors, Borders, Fonts and Shadows

Match the gallery to your site. The Style tab has these panels: Gallery, Thumbnail, Title & text, Play icon, Album filter bar and Lightbox caption.

Style the Gallery Area

This styles the area around the tiles.

  1. Open the Style tab, then Gallery.
  2. Pick a Background.
  3. Set Padding.
  4. Set a Border.
  5. Add a Shadow.

Style Each Video Tile

These apply to every video tile.

  1. In the Style tab, open Thumbnail.
  2. Set a Border.
  3. Add a Shadow.

Style the Titles

This panel shows once Show title is on.

  1. In the Style tab, open Title & text. Set the font with Typography.
  2. Set Colors.
  3. Set Padding.
Tip

Your gallery is ready. Optional next: Privacy, Analytics & Video SEO.

Pro Gallery Layouts, Titles & Playback

With Pro, the Video Gallery gets four layouts, tidier titles, an action when a video ends, and silent previews on hover.

Find them on the General tab: Layout, Display and Playback. You need an active Pro license.

Choose a Gallery Layout

  1. Select the gallery. On the General tab, open Layout.
  2. Click a card: Grid, Cards, List or Showcase.
  • Grid: videos side by side. This is the usual look.
  • Cards: each video on a card, with its title below.
  • List: one video per row, picture on the left, title on the right.
  • Showcase: a big player on top, videos below it. Click a video to play it in the big player.

For Cards, List and Showcase, switch on Show the date in Display to show when each video was published.

Tip

Set Thumbnail shape (in Layout) to 16:9 so all pictures look the same.

Place the Title Over, On Hover or Below the Video

In the Grid layout, choose where the title goes.

  1. Open Display and click a card under Title.
  • Hidden: no title. It still shows in the lightbox.
  • Over video: the title sits on the video. This is the usual choice.
  • On hover: the title shows when the mouse is over the video.
  • Below: the title sits under the video.

Keep Long Titles Short

Long titles can make tiles uneven. Title length cuts them to a few lines.

  1. In Display, choose Full, 1 line, 2 lines or 3 lines under Title length.
  2. Keep Expand cut-off titles on hover on to show the whole title when the mouse is over the video.

Show the Channel Name, Views and Likes

Show who made a video and how many people watched or liked it. This works for YouTube and Vimeo channel videos.

  1. In Display, scroll to Video details.
  2. Switch on Show channel name, Show view count or Show like count.

Vimeo shows the channel name only.

Choose What Happens When a Video Ends

  1. Open Playback.
  2. Under When a video ends, click Nothing, Next video, Replay or Button.
  • Nothing: the video just stops.
  • Next video: the next video starts by itself.
  • Replay: the video starts again.
  • Button: a button with your link shows when the video ends.

For Button, fill in:

  • Button text: the words on the button.
  • Button link: a web address that starts with https://.
  • Open the link in a new tab: switch it on to keep your page open.

These work with video files, YouTube and Vimeo, not Facebook.

Preview Videos on Hover

  1. In the Playback panel, switch on Preview on hover.

The video plays silently while the mouse is over it.

Choose the Player for YouTube and Vimeo

By default, YouTube and Vimeo play in the bPlugins player. You can use their own controls instead. It counts for every Pro block.

  1. In the left menu, open Video Gallery, then Settings.
  2. Choose a player and click Save Changes.

Ask Before Loading YouTube or Vimeo in Pro Blocks

YouTube and Vimeo may set cookies as soon as they load. Turn on a notice so visitors can agree first. It is off by default.

  1. Select the Pro block. Open its General tab.
  2. At the bottom, open Privacy.
  3. Switch on Ask before loading YouTube / Vimeo (GDPR).

Visitors now see a short notice with a Load Video button. Nothing loads from YouTube or Vimeo until they click it.

  • One click counts for the whole site.

Show a Gallery Anywhere with Shortcodes

Once one gallery looks right, you do not need to rebuild it for every page. A shortcode is a small piece of text in square brackets that tells WordPress to display a saved gallery wherever you place it.

For example, [video_gallery id=12] tells WordPress to show the gallery saved with that ID. Your number may be different.

Save a Gallery and Copy Its Shortcode

  1. Open Video Gallery in the left menu, then click Add New Video Gallery.
  2. Add your videos in the block, then click Publish.
  3. Click the copy button next to the shortcode bar at the top of the editor.

Paste the Shortcode on Another Page

  1. Edit any page and click the + button at the top left.
  2. Type Shortcode in the search box.
  3. Click the Shortcode block to add it to the page.
  4. The block appears with an empty box that says Write shortcode here. Click inside that box.
  5. Paste the line, for example [video_gallery id=12]. Your number will differ.
  6. Click Publish.
  7. Click View Page. It is in the notice at the bottom of the screen, and also as an icon in the top bar.
  8. The page opens in a new tab. The gallery now shows where you pasted the line.
Tip

In the editor, the shortcode looks like plain text. On the live page, visitors see the actual gallery. You can also copy it from the Shortcode column on the Video Gallery list page.

That is the benefit: one saved gallery can appear on several pages. Update it once, and every page using its shortcode displays the updated version. Next, make its layout and appearance fit your site in Customize Gallery Layout & Style.

Privacy, Analytics & Video SEO

Your gallery is ready. These settings are optional. They decide three things: should visitors say yes before a video loads, should plays be sent to Google Analytics, and what search engines learn about each video.

These are separate options, not a required setup sequence. Use only the ones your site needs; if a feature is not relevant, leave it off or keep its current setting.

Ask for Permission Before Loading a Video

YouTube, Vimeo and Facebook may set cookies when their players load. Enable this option if you want visitors to choose before the video player is loaded.

  1. Open the General tab, then the Privacy & SEO panel, the last panel of the tab.
  2. Switch Ask before loading YouTube / Vimeo (GDPR) on.

Visitors see a notice naming the video host. They can choose Play Video to continue or Cancel to stop, and the browser remembers their choice.

Using Video Gallery Pro? The other Pro blocks have the same switch in a Privacy panel at the end of their General tab, and one agreement counts for every video on the site. See Ask Before Loading YouTube or Vimeo in Pro Blocks.

Track Video Plays with Google Analytics

Google Analytics 4 (GA4) measures activity on your website. If GA4 is already connected, enable this option to send video-play events to it; this setting does not connect GA4 to your site by itself.

  1. In the Privacy & SEO panel, switch Send play events to Google Analytics on.

When enabled, the gallery sends the following events with the video title, host and link:

  • video_start when a visitor opens a video.
  • video_play when they press play.
  • video_watch_progress at 25, 50 and 75 percent.
  • video_watch_complete when the video ends.

To test it, open Google Analytics and go to Reports > Realtime. Then open your site in a private browser window and play a video.

Tip

Facebook videos send only video_start because Facebook does not provide the same play details. If you use Google Tag Manager, create a Custom Event trigger for each event name.

Help Search Engines Understand Your Videos

Video SEO markup is structured information added behind the page. It tells search engines details such as the video title, thumbnail and video link, helping them understand what the page contains.

Turn Video SEO Markup On or Off

This option is on by default. Turn it off only if another SEO plugin already adds video markup, so you do not create duplicate markup.

  1. Open the General tab, then the Privacy & SEO panel.
  2. Switch Video SEO markup on or off.

Give Search Engines Better Video Information

  1. Give every video a clear title.
  2. Give every video a thumbnail.
  3. Use real video links, not links to other pages.

For plugin-wide settings, demos and help resources, continue to Plugin Dashboard & Settings.

Dashboard, Settings & Pro Setup

You will do most gallery work in the page editor. Open the Video Gallery dashboard when you need plugin-wide information, demos, block controls or a setting that affects saved data.

Open the Video Gallery Dashboard

  1. Click Video Gallery in the left menu.
  2. Click Help & Demos.

Product Videos & Visitor Engagement

A video can do more than play. It can help someone understand a product, or invite them to react to what they watch. This section covers both uses: product videos first, then views, likes, saves and comments.

Before You Use WooCommerce Product Videos

WooCommerce is the WordPress plugin used to run an online store. It must be installed and active to connect a video to an existing store product. If you do not use WooCommerce, you can still enter a title, price and product link yourself.

Connect a Video to a WooCommerce Product

  1. Insert the Video Carousel Gallery block and open a video in the Videos panel.
  2. Switch on Use WooCommerce Product.
  3. Search for your product and choose it in Select Product.

Once linked, the card pulls the product title, price, sale price and image from WooCommerce. That means you do not have to update those details separately in the gallery.

Add Product Details Manually

  1. Leave Use WooCommerce Product off.
  2. Add the Product Image and the Title.
  3. Type the Current Price and the Original Price.
  4. Paste the Product URL.
  5. With WooCommerce active, click Create Product to make a store product from those details.

Adjust Product Carousel Options

  1. Type the sign before prices in Currency Symbol.
  2. Switch on Open in New Tab to keep the gallery open when the shop link is clicked.
  3. Switch on Autoplay Previews to play each card muted as it scrolls into view.

Product videos help visitors decide what to buy. If your goal is interaction instead, the Video Shorts Reel can let visitors react to a clip, save it for later or leave a comment.

Show Views, Likes, Saves & Comments

View counts show how often a video has been played. The Video Shorts Reel adds ways for visitors to respond: like a clip, save it for later or comment on it.

Show View Counts in a Playlist

Counts use short labels such as 12K views. They start at zero on your site and increase as videos are played there.

  1. Click the Video Playlist Gallery block to select it.
  2. Open Options on the General tab.
  3. Switch on View/Upload Date.

Let Visitors Like, Save and Comment on Shorts

Anyone can like a video. Visitors must be logged in to save a video or post a comment.

  1. Select the Video Shorts Reel block.
  2. Switch on Show Action Rail.

You do not need to switch on the save bookmark separately. Logged-in visitors see it on the card automatically; use the Saved button panel in the Style tab to change its appearance.

Show a Logged-In Visitor Their Saved Videos

Add [vsr_saved_videos] to a page with a Shortcode block to show the logged-in visitor a grid of videos they have saved. This is separate from the gallery shortcode: it displays that visitor's saved list, not a specific gallery.

Manage Likes and Comments

  1. Open Video Gallery and click Reel Moderation.
  2. Delete one comment, delete all comments, or reset the likes.

Common Problems and Fixes

Find your problem below and try the fix.

Pro Blocks Still Look Locked After Upgrading

Open Video Gallery > Help & Demos > Activation and check that it says Activated. See Activate Your Pro License.

My Pro License Will Not Activate

Read the message on the Activation tab. Fix the problem, for example a wrong key, and try again.

A Video Block Is Missing from the Inserter

Open Video Gallery > Help & Demos > Blocks, turn the block on, and reload the editor.

A Video Shows a Blank Thumbnail

In the Videos panel, add an image in the Thumbnail field (Poster in some Pro blocks).

A Video Will Not Autoplay with Sound

Browsers block autoplay with sound. Keep autoplay muted, or let visitors press Play.

My Feed Shows an Error

The message under the link says what is wrong. Common ones:

  • Connect YouTube first: add your free key. See Connect YouTube with a Free Key.
  • This API key is not valid. Copy it again. It starts with AIza.
  • "YouTube Data API v3" is not turned on: open the API page, click Enable, wait a minute.
  • This key has a restriction that blocks your site. In Google Cloud, set Application restrictions to None.
  • We could not find that channel or playlist. Check that the link opens, and that it is not private.
  • This is a link to one video, not a list. Paste a channel or playlist link.
  • Your site could not reach YouTube just now. Wait a minute, then click Check now.

New Videos Don't Appear Right Away

This is normal. The gallery checks at the time you chose. To update now, click Check now in How many & how often. Using a cache plugin? Clear its cache too.

My Feed Shows No Videos

The channel has no public videos, or your Shorts choice hides them all. Try All Videos under YouTube Shorts. In Pro, check your Keywords too.

YouTube Says the Daily Quota Is Used Up

Your key used up its free units for today. Videos already shown stay up. New ones appear tomorrow. Choose a longer time under Check for new videos. See Search Feeds Use More Quota.

A "Video Feed Has Stopped Updating" Notice Appeared

A feed keeps failing. Read the reason in the notice and fix it. The notice goes away by itself. See Get Told When a Feed Stops Updating.

A Video Playing in Its Tile Has Black Bars

The tile shape does not match the video. In Layout, set Thumbnail shape to 16:9 (9:16 for Shorts). See Play Videos in Their Tiles Instead.
